Serenity on your phone

Talk with Serenity and your agents on your computer from your Android phone, and answer her steps with Run or Skip, at home or anywhere else. Everything between the phone and the computer is end-to-end encrypted, and only a phone you pair on the computer can connect.

Pairing it

It needs the Serenity Framework's Settings → Phone on your computer, which comes in its next release. There, switch it on, press Pair a phone, and in the app tap Scan the code. Then say yes on the computer.

At home it connects straight to the computer. To reach it from anywhere, set up a relay in Settings → Phone before you pair: a small server you run, which passes the sealed messages along without being able to read them.
